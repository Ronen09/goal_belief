Aliased Corridor

The agent never sees its cell. It reads one noisy symbol per step (a or b, right 60 % of the time; the landmark L is exact). It first takes a few random moves, then learns its goal and has 12 moves to reach it. Each episode below is played twice from the same start, prefix and noise: once by the trained policy and once by the exact Bayes-optimal solver. The two runs match until the first move where they choose differently.

Episodes

Belief shading is the exact posterior over cells, the agent's own (shared during the prefix; after the runs diverge, each agent has its own history and belief). The ring marks the true cell, which the agent cannot see. Q* is the solver's expected discounted return (γ = 0.9) for each move under that belief. A hard case is a belief where no history score plus fixed goal bias can pick the optimal move for all three goals (round 42's additive solvability test, with this policy's own goal bias).

Matched episodes, 8,192 per policy; listed episodes are picked automatically from them. Policies (seed 0 of ten each): the round-23 reward-trained model; and two models retrained from scratch with the same PPO, seed and settings plus a supervised loss toward the optimal moves on generated post-reveal decisions, one with half of those drawn from hard cases (hard-case supervision), one from ordinary decisions only. Hard cases are judged with each policy's own goal bias.
